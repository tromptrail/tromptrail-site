Getting started

What permissions does TrompMiles need, and why?
  • Location, set to Always: to measure your drives, including when your phone is in your pocket and the app is closed.
  • Motion & Fitness: to tell driving apart from walking or cycling, so walks aren't logged as trips.
  • Notifications: to ask "Business or personal?" after each trip.
  • Camera (only if you use it): to read your odometer.

You can check or change these at any time on the Drive tab: tap the location arrow or the radio-waves button.

How does automatic trip detection work?

When you drive away, iOS wakes TrompMiles. If you're moving at driving speed, it records the trip. The start time is taken from when your iPhone's motion sensor says the drive began, and the start point is where you were parked. When you've been stopped for about three minutes, or you get out and walk, the trip is saved and you get a notification.

Stops of under five minutes, such as getting gas, stay part of one trip, and the stop is listed in the destination.

A trip was missed or ended early. What can I do?
  1. Make sure Location is set to Always and Precise Location is on (Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › TrompMiles).
  2. Keep Motion & Fitness turned on.
  3. Avoid swiping TrompMiles away in the app switcher; that can stop iOS from waking it.
  4. You can always add or fix a trip by hand: Trips tab › +.

If it keeps happening, email us the Activity Log and we'll take a look.

Vehicles

How does TrompMiles know which vehicle I'm in?

Open Vehicles › your vehicle › Car connection while sitting in the vehicle with your iPhone connected, then tap Link. Trips made while that connection is present are labeled with that vehicle.

CarPlay: iOS reports every CarPlay car the same way, so link CarPlay to only one vehicle, usually the one you drive for work. If you drive another CarPlay car now and then, the trip notification has a one-tap "Personal · [other vehicle]" button to correct it.

Bluetooth without CarPlay: each car is recognized separately.

A cable that only charges your phone can't be recognized.

I was a passenger. Will that be recorded?

Your iPhone can't tell a driver from a passenger, so rides may be recorded. They arrive in Needs Review on the Trips tab: swipe left to delete, or mark them Personal. On the Drive tab's Automatic Detection screen you can also choose what happens to drives with no linked vehicle connected: ask you, mark them personal, or don't save them.

TrompMiles Pro

What's free, and what does Pro add?

Free: manual Start/Stop trips, the full trip log, odometer readings typed by hand, and CSV export.

Pro, a one-time purchase: automatic trip detection, vehicle recognition, PDF reports, and odometer scanning with photo records. There's no subscription.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open TrompMiles, tap any Pro feature, then tap Restore Purchase.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Records and reports

Where are my trips stored? How do I back them up?

Your trips, vehicles and odometer photos are stored only on your iPhone. They're included in your normal iPhone or iCloud Backup.

Because these are tax records, we recommend also exporting a copy regularly: Reports tab › Create CSV (and, with Pro, Create PDF Report and Gather odometer photos), then Share › Save to Files or email it to yourself. Deleting the app deletes its trips, so export first.

Which report should I give my accountant?

The full-year PDF report. It lists every business trip with its date, destination, purpose and miles, and includes the Schedule C, Part IV vehicle questions. For it to show total and personal miles, enter your odometer reading for January 1 and December 31 on the Vehicles tab.

Is the deduction amount tax advice?

No. TrompMiles estimates your deduction with the IRS standard mileage rate you enter on the Reports tab. Tax rules depend on your situation; please confirm with a tax professional. TrompTrail LLC doesn't provide tax, legal or accounting advice.
