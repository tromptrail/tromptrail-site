TrompMiles

Coming soon to the App Store

The mileage log that keeps itself. TrompMiles notices when you start driving, records the trip, and asks one question when you park: business or personal?

  • Every business trip gets the details the IRS asks for: date, destination, purpose and miles.
  • Recognizes your vehicle by its CarPlay or Bluetooth connection.
  • Scan your odometer with the camera and keep the photo as a record.
  • Year, quarter or month reports as PDF or spreadsheet.
  • Your trips stay on your iPhone. No account. No subscription.

Family Calendar Companion

Coming soon to the App Store · Mac and iPhone

Everyone's calendars, together. It copies events from each person's own calendars into one shared Family calendar, and keeps it up to date.

  • You choose what each calendar shares: full details, the title only, or just "Busy."
  • Your own calendars are never changed or shared. Only copies go to Family.
  • See the family's month in color, on your Mac and iPhone.
  • Works with the Calendar app, including Google and other accounts.
  • No account, no servers, no subscription.
