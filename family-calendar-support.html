Getting started

What do I need to get started?

One person, the organizer, creates an iCloud calendar named Family in the Calendar app and shares it with each family member with View & Edit access. Then each person installs Family Calendar Companion on their own Mac, and optionally their iPhone.

You'll need macOS 14 Sonoma or later on a Mac, and iOS 17 or later on an iPhone.

How do I set it up on my Mac?
  1. Open Family Calendar Companion. When asked, choose Allow Full Access to calendars.
  2. Click its icon in the menu bar and choose Settings.
  3. Type your name, choose the Family calendar, and pick a detail level for each of your calendars.
  4. Turn on Start when I log in so your Mac keeps your part of the Family calendar up to date.
Do I need the iPhone app?

No, but it's handy. It shows the family's events once each, in a list or month view with their original colors, lets you add events, and fills in for your Mac while it's asleep. Use exactly the same name on your Mac and your iPhone.

Everyday use

Why do I see some events twice in the Calendar app?

Your own calendar and the Family calendar are both showing the same event: the original, and its copy. In the Calendar app, uncheck your own calendar in the sidebar and each event shows once, in Family.

I deleted an event and it came back. Why?

You deleted the copy in the Family calendar. Copies follow their original, so delete the event from your own calendar instead, and the copy disappears too. On iPhone, tap the event in the Family tab to open the original.

Why are all Family events one color in the Calendar app?

That's how Apple's Calendar app works: every event in a calendar uses that calendar's color. Open the Family Calendar window on your Mac, or the Family tab on iPhone, to see each event in its original calendar's color. You can switch to one color per person in Settings.

How do I add an event for the whole family?

Click + in the Family Calendar window on your Mac (or press ⌘N, or double-click a day), or tap + on iPhone, and leave the calendar set to Family. To add one of your own events, choose your own calendar instead, and it's copied into Family with your name.

What does "Busy only" share?

Only the time, shown as "Your name – Busy." The title, location and notes stay private, both in the Calendar app and in Family Calendar Companion's own views.

Troubleshooting

Nothing is syncing. What should I check?
  1. Click the menu bar icon and read the line under the app's name. If it says Paused, switch Pause off.
  2. Make sure calendar access is on: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars › Family Cal › Full Access. On iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars › Family Cal.
  3. Click Sync Now. Recent activity shows what changed.
Someone's events aren't showing.

Their events are copied from their own Mac, so it needs to be on and awake. A sleeping Mac catches up when it wakes. iCloud can take a few minutes. And in their Settings, check that the calendar isn't set to Don't share.

The calendar list looks wrong.

Click Refresh in Settings. If you just accepted a shared calendar, give iCloud a minute first. If an orange notice says a calendar no longer exists, click Remove.

An orange notice says removals are held for review.

Large batches of removals are held as a safety check. If you just stopped sharing a calendar, click Remove Them. If you weren't expecting it, wait a few minutes and click Sync Now; if iCloud was catching up, the notice clears on its own.

I can't add events to the Family calendar.

Ask the organizer to share the Family calendar with you as View & Edit.

On iPhone, the Family tab is out of date.

iOS lets apps run in the background only now and then. Open the app and it syncs right away. Keep Background App Refresh on for Family Cal in Settings › General › Background App Refresh.

Leaving or pausing

How do I stop sharing, or leave?

To pause, switch on Pause in the menu. To stop sharing one calendar, set it to Don't share in Settings. To leave, open Settings › Leaving › Remove My Events from Family, on each device you use, then delete the app. Your own calendars are never changed.

Purchases

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app's Settings and tap Restore Purchase.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
