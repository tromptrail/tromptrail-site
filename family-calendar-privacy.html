Family Calendar Companion

Privacy policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version: your calendars stay on your devices and in your iCloud account. TrompTrail LLC doesn't collect, store, sell or share your personal information. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app does with your calendars

Family Calendar Companion reads the calendars you choose in its Settings and writes copies of their events into the shared calendar you pick as your Family calendar. It does this on your Mac and iPhone, through Apple's Calendar system. None of your calendar data is sent to TrompTrail LLC. We have no servers that receive your data, and we can't see it.

Your settings (your name, your chosen calendars and their detail levels) are stored only on your device.

Who can see the copies

The copies go into your Family calendar, so anyone that calendar is shared with can see them, in the Calendar app and in Family Calendar Companion. You decide how much each of your calendars shares: full details, the title only, or just "Busy." Your own calendars are never changed or shared by the app.

Each copy carries a small hidden note in the event's link field so the app can recognize it. It contains your name as you typed it, a reference to the original event, a short code for the device that made the copy, and the original calendar's color. Nothing else.

What the app uses on your devices

Apple services the app relies on

Family Calendar Companion uses services built into macOS and iOS. They are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us:

  • iCloud Calendar, which stores and shares your calendars, including the Family calendar.
  • The App Store, for any in-app purchase. We receive no payment details or personal information from Apple.
  • TestFlight, if you test a pre-release version. Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us.

Calendars from other providers

If you've added Google, Microsoft or other calendar accounts to the Calendar app, their events are stored by those providers under their own privacy policies. Family Calendar Companion only reads them through your Mac or iPhone, and sends nothing to those providers itself.

Analytics and advertising

Family Calendar Companion contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Families may use the app together, including children whose calendars are shared with the family. The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Family Calendar Companion handles information, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
