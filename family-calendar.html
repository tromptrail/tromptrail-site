Family Calendar Companion

Coming soon to the App Store

Everyone's calendars, together. Family Calendar Companion copies events from each person's own calendars into one shared Family calendar, and keeps it up to date.

What it does

  • One Family calendar for everyone. Events from each person's calendars appear in it, labeled with their name.
  • You choose what's shared, calendar by calendar: full details, just the title, or simply "Busy."
  • Your calendars stay yours. Only copies go to Family; your own calendars are never changed or shared.
  • Colors kept. The Family view on Mac and iPhone shows each event in the color of the calendar it came from.
  • Works with the Calendar app on your Mac and iPhone, including Google and other accounts you've added there.
  • Private by design. No accounts, no servers, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your devices and in iCloud.

Requirements

A Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later for each person who shares calendars, and optionally an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. An iCloud calendar named Family, shared with everyone with View & Edit access.
