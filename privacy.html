TrompMiles

Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version: your trips stay on your iPhone. TrompTrail LLC doesn't collect, store, sell or share your personal information. There's no account, no advertising and no tracking.

What TrompMiles keeps, and where

Everything you record in TrompMiles is stored only on your iPhone: trips (dates, times, places, distances, purposes and categories), vehicles, odometer readings and photos, and the app's Activity Log. None of it is sent to TrompTrail LLC. We have no servers that receive your data, and we can't see it.

Your iPhone includes this data in your device or iCloud Backup, which you control through Apple. When you delete TrompMiles, its data is deleted from your iPhone.

What the app uses on your iPhone

PermissionWhat it's used for
Location (Always, or While Using)Measuring the distance and route of your drives, and noticing when you start a drive.
Motion & FitnessTelling driving apart from walking or cycling, and finding when a drive began.
CameraReading your odometer, only when you tap the camera button. Text is read on your iPhone. Photos are kept only if you choose to save them.

Audio connection namesRecognizing your vehicle by the name of its CarPlay or Bluetooth connection. TrompMiles never records audio or uses your microphone.

Apple services the app relies on

TrompMiles uses a few services built into iOS. They are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us:

  • Apple Maps, to turn a trip's start and end points into street addresses and to measure road distances. To do this, iOS sends those map coordinates to Apple.
  • The App Store, for the optional TrompMiles Pro purchase. We receive no payment details or personal information from Apple.
  • Shortcuts, if you choose to set up Bluetooth automations. These run on your iPhone.

Sharing

Information leaves TrompMiles only when you choose to send it, for example by sharing a CSV, PDF report or odometer photos to Files, Mail or another app. Where it goes after that is up to you and that app.

Analytics and advertising

TrompMiles contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children

TrompMiles is meant for adult drivers and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how TrompMiles handles information, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
